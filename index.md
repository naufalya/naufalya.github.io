## 🔧 System Requirements

To run rewindDV smoothly, you’ll need the following:

- **A Mac**: Any Mac with a FireWire 400 or FireWire 800 port. If your Mac is newer and lacks FireWire, you may need a FireWire to Thunderbolt adapter (available online).
- **macOS Version**: rewindDV supports macOS 10.15 (Catalina) and newer. If you’re running an older version, consider updating your system first.
- **A Camcorder**: Any MiniDV, Digital8, or HDV camcorder with a FireWire (iLink or IEEE 1394) port.
- **A FireWire Cable**: Standard 4-pin to 6-pin FireWire cable usually works for most camcorders. Some require a 4-pin to 4-pin cable—check your camcorder’s manual.
- **Storage Space**: A one-hour DV tape uses about 12 GB of space, and HDV uses about 6 GB per hour. Make sure you have enough free space on your hard drive.

## 🛠️ Troubleshooting Tips

Even with a smooth setup, you might run into a few hiccups. Here are simple solutions to common problems:

- **Camcorder Not Detected**: Make sure your camcorder is turned on and set to "Play" or "VCR" mode. Also, try plugging the FireWire cable into a different port on your Mac.
- **Audio or Video Gaps**: Ensure your tape is clean and rewound fully. Sometimes scratches or dirt on the tape cause issues—fast-forward and rewind once to clear the tape path.
- **Slow Capture**: FireWire is fast, but if you’re capturing HDV, it may take a bit longer. Be patient—it’s normal.
- **Permission Errors**: If your Mac blocks rewindDV, go to System Settings > Privacy & Security and click "Open Anyway" next to the rewindDV entry.

## ❓ Frequently Asked Questions

**Do I need a special adapter?**
Only if your Mac doesn’t have a FireWire port. Many newer Macs require a FireWire to Thunderbolt adapter, which costs around $20–30 online.

**Can I use rewindDV with a Windows computer?**
No, rewindDV is designed specifically for macOS. If you use Windows, you may want to look for other tools, but we recommend rewindDV for Mac users because of its quality and ease of use.

**What about tapes from other formats?**
rewindDV focuses on DV, Digital8, and HDV tapes. If you have VHS or other analog formats, you’d need a conversion service—but for digital camcorder tapes, this is the right tool.

**Will rewindDV damage my tapes?**
Not at all. The capture process is gentle and doesn’t touch the physical tape. Your tapes remain exactly as they were, and you get a perfect digital copy.
